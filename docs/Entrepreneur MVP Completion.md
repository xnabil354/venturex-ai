Approved scope: BRD BR-003/009/012/015/016/017, PRD entrepreneur journey, FSD risk, compare and startup roadmap. The active source is private owner-scoped hypotheses, not the archived published catalog.

- Require preferredProductType on create/update for entrepreneurs; legacy profiles remain readable and receive an edit-profile action when no exploration query is present.
- Risk policy `entrepreneur-risk-v1`: weighted pressure from confirmed budget fit (30%), execution feasibility (20%), competitor sample (20%), search stability (15%), experience (7.5%) and time (7.5%). Pressure = 100 minus the corresponding available factor. At least three factors, 60% coverage, known budget and one market factor are required. Weighted available pressure >=60 is HIGH, >=30 MEDIUM, otherwise LOW only at 100% coverage; incomplete low-pressure evidence remains unknown. This is preliminary preparation risk, not overall business failure probability. Derive from the stored analysis, including historical snapshots, without changing archived scores.
- Compare 2–3 distinct owner-scoped analyzed hypotheses, without paid inference; show scores, coverage, model, dates, source geography, target, competition, risk, confirmed capital, needs and evidence. Do not rank mixed versions/coverage/geographies automatically.
- Finder and saved-list pages use bounded 12-item pages, stable createdAt/id order, next/previous links. Dashboard previews remain bounded independently.
- Startup roadmap requires an entrepreneur-owned permanently saved hypothesis with valid requirements and both numeric scores. Generate six deterministic validation stages using the archived concept, target, requirements, financial estimate and evidence. No fabricated costs, sales or guarantees. Idempotent creation under a row lock, server-created date, linked source fingerprint.
- Store optional startupRoadmap metadata alongside analysis in hypothesis JSONB, validated separately. This adds no column/table/FK and never changes archived analysis. Read via /opportunities/hypotheses/:id/roadmap; library on /roadmaps includes private records with pagination. Existing catalog roadmaps remain accessible through their current detail routes.

## Recovery and partial-evidence roadmap refinement
Approved 21 October 2026: valid Compare URLs retain owner-scoped available analyses even if another selected record is absent or no longer score-eligible. Show a neutral recovery notice, remove only unavailable requested IDs from that owner's session selection, and offer the list when fewer than two remain. Missing and foreign-owner records are indistinguishable; malformed input/auth failures retain existing guards. Storage/read errors are not treated as deletions.
New generator personalized-v3 recognizes missing and sparse history (under 40 valid observations, 10 observed months, 270 days, or missing momentum/stability factors), unknown experience/time/channel requirements, and existing mismatches. Priorities use weighted deficit (100 - factor value) times its stored score weight, descending, with stable key tie-break; across Opportunity/Fit this is an action-planning heuristic, not a calibrated risk model. Unknown factors are explicit completion tasks, never zero scores. No scores are changed.
Optional analysis.planningContext.timeCommitment snapshots profile capacity for new analyses. All six relative stage timelines add LIMITED/PART_TIME/FULL_TIME pacing; legacy snapshots ask to confirm hours rather than infer capacity from requiredTime or current profile. Timelines do not invent deadlines. v2 and older roadmap archives remain valid and unchanged; no regeneration/migration/provider call is introduced.
